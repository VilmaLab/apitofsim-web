Transmission model

The mass spectrogram above is based on the assumption encoding in the model that all particles entering the aparatus either escape, reaching the sensor or fragment and then the fragments reach the sensor.

In reality most particles do not reach the sensor. Although our model doesn't take into account this, it is possible to fit a simple "transmission" model. The idea is to take historical data (TODO: EXPLAIN THIS FURTHER) and fit a model to predict the yield based on particle mass.
